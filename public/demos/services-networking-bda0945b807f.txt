Complete the task
$ IP=$(kubectl get service nginx -o jsonpath='{.spec.clusterIP}')
kubectl run client --image=busybox:1.37 --restart=Never --command -- wget -qO- "$IP"
kubectl wait --for=jsonpath='{.status.phase}'=Succeeded pod/client --timeout=90s
kubectl logs client > answer.txt

Why the repair works
A Service selects Pods by labels and maps a stable service port to a container endpoint. Verify actual traffic as well as the object fields; a Service with no usable endpoints cannot deliver requests.
