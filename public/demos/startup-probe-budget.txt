Complete the task
$ cat > 'slow.yaml' <<'KUBEQUEST_FILE'
apiVersion: v1
kind: Pod
metadata:
  name: slow

spec:
  containers:

      image: busybox:1.37

        - sh

        exec:

kubectl delete pod slow -n kq-startup

Why the repair works
Until a startup probe succeeds, Kubernetes holds back liveness and readiness probes. After startup succeeds, liveness detects a broken process and readiness controls traffic eligibility. The startup failureThreshold × periodSeconds gives an approximate failure budget, not an exact stopwatch.
