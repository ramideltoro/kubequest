Complete the task
$ kubectl patch service nginx --type=merge -p '{"spec":{"type":"NodePort"}}'
NODE=$(kubectl get nodes -o json | jq -r '[.items[0].status.addresses[] | select(.type == "InternalIP" and (.address | contains(":") | not))][0].address')
PORT=$(kubectl get service nginx -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0].nodePort}')
curl -fsS --retry 5 --retry-connrefused --retry-delay 1 "http://$NODE:$PORT" > answer.txt

Why the repair works
A Service selects Pods by labels and maps a stable service port to a container endpoint. Verify actual traffic as well as the object fields; a Service with no usable endpoints cannot deliver requests.
