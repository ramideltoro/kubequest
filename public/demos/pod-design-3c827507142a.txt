Bound a Job’s running time

Actual commands and output captured in a disposable KubeQuest Kubernetes lab. Video is a condensed replay; the full transcript follows.

Inspect the starting environment
$ kubectl get pods,deployments,services,jobs -n quest
No resources found in quest namespace.


Complete the task
$ kubectl apply -f - <<'KUBEQUEST_YAML'
apiVersion: batch/v1
kind: Job
metadata:
  name: deadline
spec:
  activeDeadlineSeconds: 30
  template:
    spec:
      restartPolicy: Never
      containers:
        - name: worker
          image: busybox:1.37
          command:
            - sleep
            - "3600"
KUBEQUEST_YAML
kubectl wait --for=condition=Failed job/deadline --timeout=90s
job.batch/deadline created
job.batch/deadline condition met


Run behavioral checks
$ KubeQuest: Check my work
PASS The deadline is configured
PASS The Job stopped at its deadline

Why the repair works
activeDeadlineSeconds bounds the Job’s total active time. It is different from retry limits or CronJob scheduling deadlines.
