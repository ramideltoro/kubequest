Complete the task
$ kubectl apply -f - <<'KUBEQUEST_YAML'
apiVersion: networking.k8s.io/v1
kind: NetworkPolicy
metadata:
  name: web-only
spec:
  podSelector:
    matchLabels:
      app: nginx
  policyTypes:
    - Ingress
  ingress:
    - from:
        - podSelector:
            matchLabels:
              access: granted
      ports:
        - protocol: TCP
          port: 80
KUBEQUEST_YAML
sleep 3

Why the repair works
NetworkPolicies are additive. Selecting the server Pods isolates their ingress, and the allowed peer selector opens only the intended client set. Always test both allowed and denied paths.
